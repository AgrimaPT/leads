# Leads – Lead Management & Sales Follow-up System

Leads is a web-based application designed to help businesses manage customer leads, track follow-ups, monitor lead status and prepare quotations.

## Features

### Lead Management
- Enter and maintain customer lead details.
- Track leads throughout the sales process.
- Update lead status based on progress.

### Follow-up Management
- Maintain follow-up records for customer leads.
- Track the progress of customer interactions.

### Quotation Management
- Create quotations for customer leads.
- Manage quotations as part of the sales process.

## Application Overview

1. Add customer lead information to the application.
2. Maintain follow-up records for each lead.
3. Update lead status as the sales process progresses.
4. Prepare quotations for customers.

## Technology Stack

- **Backend:** Python, Django
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
- **Database:** SQLite
- **Deployment:** AWS EC2, Linux, Gunicorn, Nginx, SSL/HTTPS
